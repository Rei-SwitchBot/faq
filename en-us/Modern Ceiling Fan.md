### Troubleshoot a one-hour schedule difference on Modern Ceiling Fan in British Columbia

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 20

**Applies to:** Modern Ceiling Fan

Most of British Columbia, Canada, did not turn clocks back on November 1, 2026. If your ceiling fan turns on or off an hour earlier or later than scheduled, switch off the power to the fan, then switch it back on.

If the schedule is still off by an hour, [contact SwitchBot Support through the app](https://support.switch-bot.com/hc/en-us/articles/33198242544151). Include your fan model, device ID, and city. Tell us the scheduled time and the local time the fan actually turned on or off.
