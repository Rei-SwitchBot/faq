### Troubleshooting SwitchBot Garage Door Opener Keeps Disconnecting from Wi-Fi

Thank you for reaching out to us. We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused and appreciate the opportunity to assist you.
Regarding this issue, could you please check the following points?
1. Please place the Garage Door Opener closer to the Wi-Fi router (within 5 meters).
2. Unplug the Wi-Fi router, then plug it back in.
3. Please set up Wi-Fi again by going to SwitchBot app -> Garage Door Opener -> Gear icon -> Wi-Fi settings near the device.
4. If the device goes offline at a certain time, please check if the problem can be resolved by setting DHCP to "Never expire: in the following order: Wi-Fi router basic settings → Settings → IPV4 → DHCP → Never expire.
5. If your Wi-Fi router supports both 5GHz and 2.4GHz frequencies, connect to a 2.4GHz network.
*If your Wi-Fi router is set to use the 2.4GHz band for 802.11 b/g/n and has ax enabled, please disable ax.

### What Should I Do if Garage Door Opener Cannot Be Found in Matter, Alexa, or Google Home?

Last Updated: 2026 Aug 19

- **Related Products: Garage Door Opener**

1. Make sure the device and your third-party home hub, such as Apple HomePod or Apple TV, are connected to the same 2.4 GHz network and have a stable network connection.
2. Make sure IPv6 is enabled properly on your router.
3. Try restarting your home hub.
4. Try restarting your router.
5. If the device is unresponsive in Apple Home, try creating a new Apple Home environment and check whether the issue improves: Create a new Home in the Apple Home app.
6. Add any Apple home hub to the new Home.
7. Reset the device and add it to the new Home, then check whether it works properly.

### Why Does Garage Door Opener Show as a Door Lock in HomeKit, Alexa, or Google Home?

Last Updated: 2026 Aug 14

- **Related Products: Garage Door Opener**

Since Garage Door Opener works similarly to a door lock management device in actual use, these platforms identify it as a door lock device.

---
### Why Can Garage Door Opener Only Be Used as a Condition, Not an Action, in Automations?

Last Updated: 2026 Aug 14

- **Related Products: Garage Door Opener**

For customer safety and to prevent the garage door from being opened accidentally due to unintended operations, Garage Door Opener is currently restricted from being used as an automation action.

---
### Why Can Siri Only Close Garage Door Opener but Not Open It?

Last Updated: 2026 Aug 14

- **Related Products: Garage Door Opener**

For customer safety and to prevent the garage door from being opened accidentally due to unintended voice commands, we currently restrict opening the garage door directly through Siri.

Please use Siri to close the garage door only when it is safe to do so.
