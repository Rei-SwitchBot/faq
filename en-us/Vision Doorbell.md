## SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

---
### Does SwitchBot Vision Doorbell work outdoors at night?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

Yes. SwitchBot Vision Doorbell is rated IP65 for outdoor use and operates from −20°C to 45°C.

In the dark, use infrared night vision or the white LED for a color view. Night vision reaches up to 7.5 m (about 25 ft) in ideal conditions.

---
### How long does the SwitchBot Vision Doorbell battery last?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

SwitchBot Vision Doorbell can run for about 100 days on its built-in 5,000 mAh battery alone, without the included external battery. This estimate assumes 10 rings, 10 face recognition unlocks, and 10 locks using the button each day, with 5-second video clips for ring and unlock events. Battery life varies with the Wi-Fi connection mode and how often you use these features.

- Below 30%, doorbell calls, video, and QR code unlocking are unavailable.
- Below 20%, face recognition unlocking also stops. You can still unlock with an NFC card.

---
### Does SwitchBot Vision Doorbell work with 5 GHz Wi-Fi?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

No. To set up and use SwitchBot Vision Doorbell, connect it to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

---
### Does SwitchBot Vision Doorbell save recordings to the cloud or to an SD card?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

SwitchBot Vision Doorbell saves video recordings to the cloud only. It doesn’t support SD cards. In Europe, you need an AI Guard subscription to save video recordings.

---
### Does SwitchBot Vision Doorbell let me see and talk to visitors from my phone?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

Yes. When someone presses the button on SwitchBot Vision Doorbell, you can see and talk to them in the SwitchBot app.

To check the live view when no one has rung, set SwitchBot Vision Doorbell to stay connected to Wi-Fi.

---
### Does SwitchBot Vision Doorbell let me unlock my SwitchBot lock remotely without a hub?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell and SwitchBot Lock series

Yes. You need a SwitchBot lock paired with SwitchBot Vision Doorbell. Once SwitchBot Vision Doorbell is connected to Wi-Fi, you can unlock the paired lock from the SwitchBot app while you’re away. No separate hub is needed.

---
### Does SwitchBot Vision Doorbell let me give guests a temporary QR code?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

Yes. Create a guest QR code in the SwitchBot app and choose when it starts working and when it expires. The code can be valid for up to 7 days.

Anyone who receives the code can forward it. Set the code to work only during the visit, and delete it when you no longer need it.

---
### Does SwitchBot Vision Doorbell work with my SwitchBot lock?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

Yes. SwitchBot Vision Doorbell works with all models in the SwitchBot Lock series.

---
### How can I unlock my door with SwitchBot Vision Doorbell?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 25

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Vision Doorbell

Pair SwitchBot Vision Doorbell with a SwitchBot lock, then use face recognition, an NFC card, or a QR code to unlock your door.
