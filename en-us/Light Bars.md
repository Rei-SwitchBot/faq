## SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

---
### Can the Two RGBICWW Light Bars Be Controlled Separately or Display Different Colors?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

You can use the segment settings to assign different colors to each light bar.

However, the two light bars cannot be turned on or off independently. They can only be turned on or off together.

---
### Do RGBICWW Light Bars Support Music Sync and TV Screen Sync?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

RGBICWW Light Bars support **Music Mode** only. Music Mode synchronizes the lighting effects with sound and is different from TV screen synchronization.

TV screen synchronization is not supported.

---
### Can RGBICWW Light Bars Be Placed Horizontally, Vertically, or Behind a Monitor?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

Yes. RGBICWW Light Bars support horizontal placement, vertical placement, adhesive mounting, and magnetic mounting. Desktop and monitor mounting brackets are also included.

Before choosing an installation method, check the mounting surface, available securing points, and cable-routing space. Magnetic mounting can only be used on surfaces suitable for magnetic attachment.

---
### Is the Maximum Brightness 510 lm or 800 lm?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

Both values are correct under different lighting conditions:

- **Up to 800 lm:** Both light bars are on, with warm white and cool white LEDs operating together to produce mixed white light.
- **Up to 510 lm:** Cool white mode only.

---
### Why Is Brightness Adjustment with the Controller Less Precise Than in the App?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

The controller adjusts brightness in 10 levels, while the SwitchBot App provides continuous adjustment using a slider. These controls use different adjustment methods. For finer brightness control, use the SwitchBot App.

---
### Is the Device Defective If One Light Bar or Part of It Does Not Light Up?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

1. Check the brightness and zone settings in the SwitchBot App. Some lighting effects may intentionally create dark areas.
2. Disconnect the power and check that all detachable connectors are securely connected.
3. Switch between several static lighting modes. If the same area remains unlit or displays abnormally in every mode, record a video showing the issue. via **Feedback** in the SwitchBot App.
4. Do not disassemble the light bars yourself.

---
### What Should I Do If RGBICWW Light Bars Do Not Light Up?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

1. Make sure the power outlet is working.
2. Use the original compatible power adapter.
3. Check whether the lights are turned off or their brightness is set too low.
4. Disconnect the power and check that all connectors are fully and securely inserted. Reconnect the power and test the lights again.

---
### What Should I Do If the App Cannot Find or Connect to RGBICWW Light Bars?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

1. Make sure the device is properly connected to power.
2. Update the SwitchBot App to the latest version and grant the phone permissions required during device setup.
3. Follow the user manual to put the device into pairing mode.
4. Move your phone close to the device, then try adding it again.
5. When connecting to Wi-Fi, make sure you are using a 2.4 GHz network and that the Wi-Fi password is correct.

---
### Do RGBICWW Light Bars Support 5 GHz Wi-Fi?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

No. RGBICWW Light Bars support 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only and do not support 5 GHz Wi-Fi. Please connect them to your router’s 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

---
### Where Can I Use RGBICWW Light Bars? Can They Be Used for General Lighting?

Last Updated: 2026 Sep 28

- **Related Products:** SwitchBot RGBICWW Light Bars

RGBICWW Light Bars are primarily designed for gaming desks, entertainment areas, bedrooms, and everyday workspaces. They provide both colorful ambient lighting and supplemental white lighting.

Whether they can provide sufficient lighting for a work surface depends on their placement, lighting direction, and the ambient brightness of the room. Total luminous flux alone cannot determine whether they can replace professional task or general lighting.
