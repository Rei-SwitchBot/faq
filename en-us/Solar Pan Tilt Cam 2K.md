## SwitchBot Solar Pan Tilt Cam 2K

---
### How Do I Update the Firmware? What Should I Do if an Update Fails?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

1. Firmware updates are automatically made available. You can update manually in the SwitchBot App or enable Auto Update, which updates the device automatically at night.
2. If the update fails, move the device closer to the router and try again.

---
### What Should I Do if the Image Is Blurry or Out of Focus?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

1. Make sure the protective film has been removed from the outdoor unit lens.
2. If the lens is dirty, gently wipe it with a lint-free cloth.
3. Temperature changes may cause condensation. The condensation will disappear after the temperature stabilizes.

---
### Why Is There No Sound or Very Low Sound From the Camera or Monitor?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

1. Adjust the volume for both devices in the SwitchBot App.
2. Make sure the microphone openings are not blocked by tape or other objects.

---
### Why Does the Indoor Unit Not Display Video When I Press the Call Button?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

The router may be too far from the camera or monitor. The maximum transmission distance is 40 m. Walls, metal doors, and other obstacles can significantly reduce the effective transmission distance.

---
### What Should I Do if the Device Cannot Connect to the Network?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

1. Remove and add the device again in the SwitchBot App, or reset its WLAN settings and follow the setup instructions.
2. Make sure the device connects to an 802.11b/g/n, 2.4 GHz, IPv4 Wi-Fi network.
3. Keep the router within 40 m of the doorbell and camera.
4. If your router uses a MAC address allowlist, add the monitor's MAC address to the allowlist or disable this feature.

---
### Can the Solar Panel Be Removed? Does It Need to Be Purchased Separately?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

The solar panel can be installed separately or mounted on the camera. It is included as a standard accessory and does not need to be purchased separately.

---
### Installation Environment Recommendations

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

Keep the indoor and outdoor units within 40 m of each other, with no more than two walls between them.

Do not place the indoor unit on a metal table.

---
### What Protocol Is Used Between the Indoor and Outdoor Units? What Functions Are Available if Wi-Fi Disconnects After Setup?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

The indoor and outdoor units communicate through a private protocol.

1. You can press the Call button on the indoor unit to start live view.
2. Two-way audio between the indoor and outdoor units remains available.

---
### How Many Outdoor Units Can Be Added to One Indoor Unit? Can an Outdoor Unit Be Used Without an Indoor Unit?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

One indoor unit can be paired with up to eight outdoor units.

An outdoor unit must be used with an indoor unit and cannot be used on its own.

---
### What Power Supply Methods Are Supported for the Outdoor and Indoor Units?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

1. The outdoor unit has a built-in 13,500 mAh lithium battery. After fully charging it with a 5 V/2 A power adapter, it can be installed and used wirelessly.
2. The indoor unit supports a continuous power supply of 100–240 V~, 50/60 Hz.

---
### Is the Device Waterproof? Can It Be Used in Harsh Weather?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 11

Related Product:** Solar Pan/Tilt Cam 2K

1. The outdoor unit is rated IP66 for water and dust resistance. Its operating temperature range is -20°C to 45°C, with an operating humidity of 95% RH or below. It can be used in harsh weather conditions. However, when using it in heavy rain or snow, we recommend installing a protective cover to prevent direct, prolonged exposure to rain or snow, which may damage the device.
2. The indoor unit is for indoor use only and is not waterproof. Its operating temperature range is -15°C to 45°C, with an operating humidity of 95% RH or below.
