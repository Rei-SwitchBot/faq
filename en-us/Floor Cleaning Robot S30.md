### How do I clean multiple floors with S30?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 3

Related Product: Floor Cleaning Robot S30

Update the app and firmware, then enable No Station Mode (Beta) in SwitchBot App > Floor Cleaning Robot S30 > Cleaning Mode. After the robot fills with water, carry it to the target floor. When cleaning is complete, carry it back to the station floor to empty dust and dry the mop in the app. If the clean-water box runs out in No Station Mode, the task stops. The robot may announce a failed return because there is no station on that floor; this is not a malfunction.

With optional Water Stations on every floor, mark each station in the app. After cleaning, the robot can go to a marked Water Station for draining and mop cleaning.

If that floor has no Multi-Functional Station, return the robot to the station floor for dust collection and drying.

### How to Connect Floor Cleaning Robot S30 to Wi-Fi and Mark the Water Station

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 18

Related Product:** Floor Cleaning Robot S30

- **SwitchBot App Version:** V10.11 or newer

1. Make sure that the SwitchBot App is up to date.
2. Turn on the Floor Cleaning Robot S30 Multi-Functional Station.
3. Connect the device to Wi-Fi as follows: Open the SwitchBot App and sign in.
4. Tap the + icon in the upper-right corner, then tap **Add Device**.
5. Select **Floor Cleaning Robot S30**.
6. Press and hold the robot's **Return to Station** button and **Mode Switch** button for 3 seconds. The S30 will announce, “Wi-Fi has been reset.”
7. Tap **Connect Device**.
8. The device will be detected automatically.
9. Enter the SSID and password for a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, then tap **Next**.
10. When the network connection is complete, an “Added” message will appear. You can then set the device name and room.
11. Mark the Water Station using either method below.

**Method 1:**

- Carry the S30 to the Water Station and manually dock it with the station.
- Wait for the robot to announce, “The robot has entered Bluetooth pairing mode,” followed by, “Paired with the Water Station successfully.”
- Briefly press the robot's **Return to Station** button. The robot will automatically mark the Water Station.

**Method 2:**

- Carry the S30 to the Water Station and manually dock it with the station.
- Wait for the robot to announce, “The robot has entered Bluetooth pairing mode,” followed by, “Paired with the Water Station successfully.”
- Open the SwitchBot App and select **Floor Cleaning Robot S30**.
- Tap **Device Features** in the lower-left corner.
- Tap **Settings**.
- Tap **Mark Water Station Location**.
- Drag the water-drop icon to the Water Station location and select its orientation.
- Tap **Mark**.
- S30 will automatically navigate to the Water Station to complete the marking process.

### What should I do if cleaning performance is poor on a dark carpet?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 3

Related Product: Floor Cleaning Robot S30

The cliff sensor may mistake a dark carpet for a drop and stop the robot. In the SwitchBot App, go to Floor Cleaning Robot S30 > Map Editing > Carpet Cleaning > Carpet Settings, and mark the relevant area as a dark carpet. Do not use this setting near stairs; mark those areas as restricted zones instead, because cliff detection is reduced in dark-carpet areas.

### Is it normal for the center opening of the clean-water box to drip after removal?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 3

Related Product: Floor Cleaning Robot S30

If the clean-water box is nearly full, a small amount of water may drip from the center opening because of the siphon effect. Reinstall the box promptly. This does not affect normal operation.

### What should I do if the robot cannot dock or repeatedly undocks from the station?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 3

Related Product: Floor Cleaning Robot S30

Dirty infrared lenses or charging contacts can affect docking, especially after water filling or draining. Wipe the infrared lenses and contacts on the rear of the robot and on the Multi-Functional Station or Water Station with a clean soft cloth or alcohol wipe. Dock the robot again and check that it connects normally. Inspect and clean these areas regularly after using water functions.

### Can I use Floor Cleaning Robot S30 overseas?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 6

Related Product: Floor Cleaning Robot S30

Use a model that matches the local voltage: Japan 100 V, 50/60 Hz; United States 120 V, 60 Hz; Europe 220-240 V, 50/60 Hz. When using a purchased unit abroad, use a transformer compatible with the local voltage. Using an incompatible voltage may cause a short circuit in the station. The product contains a lithium battery; check airline rules before carrying it on board.

### What Should I Do if Floor Cleaning Robot S30 Reports a Water Filling Error or Cannot Add Water?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 4

Related Product:** Floor Cleaning Robot S30

When Using the S30 Multi-Functional Station

1. Check that the clean-water docking silicone plug is correctly installed and not visibly damaged.![](https://support.switch-bot.com/hc/article_attachments/44642729212695)
2. Make sure the clean-water tank contains water and is installed correctly. When the robot begins filling with water at the station, firmly push the robot toward the rear to ensure it is fully docked with the station.

**Troubleshooting tips:**

- Keep the robot powered on. Press and hold the Mode button for 10 seconds until you hear a chime.
- Press the Mode button once. The water pump will start and make a sound. As shown in the video, firmly press and hold the water inlet opening for 3 to 4 seconds, then check for airflow or suction.![](https://support.switch-bot.com/hc/article_attachments/44642700670359)
- If you cannot hear the water pump, make sure the buttons were not pressed incorrectly. Repeat the steps two or three times.
- When finished, press and hold the Power button to restart the robot.

[Restart S30 video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PqQl2m-XO4sYf4K_xM7csuTuNG9zKHJM/view?usp=drive_link)

When Using the S30 Water Station

1. Check that the clean-water docking silicone plug is correctly installed and not visibly damaged.
2. Make sure the clean-water hose is correctly installed, undamaged, and not bent or kinked. Also confirm that the household water supply valve is open.![](https://support.switch-bot.com/hc/article_attachments/44642700670743)
3. When the robot begins filling with water at the station, firmly push the robot toward the rear to ensure it is fully docked with the station.
4. Check whether the S30 clean-water pump is working properly by following the same steps above.

### How do I set up Matter for S30 on iOS?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 3

Related Product: Floor Cleaning Robot S30

Requirements: SwitchBot App V10.11 or later, an iPhone, and a HomePod or Apple TV. Add S30 to the SwitchBot App and connect S30, the iPhone, and the home hub to the same 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. In the SwitchBot App, open S30 > Settings > Matter Setup and copy the pairing code. In the Apple Home app, tap Add Accessory > More Options > My Accessory Isn't Shown Here > Enter Setup Code, paste the code, and complete the setup. With iOS 18.4 or later, Matter setup is available regardless of the home-hub model.
