### How do I control and schedule devices by voice?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 24

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Hub 4

In the SwitchBot app, open Hub 4 → Settings → Voice Settings, agree to the privacy policy and enable AI Voice Dialogue. Turn on the Hub 4 microphone.

Wake Hub 4, then say the device name and the action or mode you want. Including both helps Hub 4 understand your command.

- “Turn on the bedroom light.”
- “Switch the living room air purifier to Sleep Mode.”

Wake Hub 4 and say when you want a specific device to turn on or off. For example:

- “Turn on the bedroom light tomorrow at 8 a.m.”
- “Turn off the living room light in ten minutes.”

If Hub 4 asks which device you mean, say the room or device name. If you have several devices, Hub 4 may ask you to clarify. You can control several devices in one request. Keep the list short.

- Turn on the bedroom light.
- Put the living room air purifier in sleep mode.
- Turn on the bedroom light at 8 AM tomorrow.
- Turn off the living room lights in 10 minutes.

### How to Control Fire TV With SwitchBot Hub 4

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 18

Related Product: SwitchBot Hub 4

Hub 4 works as a Bluetooth remote for Fire TV. After pairing, you can control Fire TV with the Hub 4 screen, knob, buttons or your voice. This feature is currently in Beta.

Add Fire TV in the SwitchBot App

1. Open the SwitchBot App and tap Hub 4.
2. Under Button Settings, tap Manage Screen > Manage Devices.
3. Tap + in the upper right corner, then tap Third-party Control > Fire TV (Beta).
4. Enter a name and tap Save.

[https://support.switch-bot.com/hc/article_attachments/45117856608407](https://support.switch-bot.com/hc/article_attachments/45117856608407)

Pair Hub 4 With Fire TV

1. On your Fire TV, select the Settings icon, then go to Controllers and Bluetooth Devices > Other Bluetooth Devices.
2. Select SwitchBot_xxxx.
3. In the app, tap Pair Now and wait for pairing to complete.
4. On the Edit Device page, tap Save. Fire TV now appears under Manage Screen > Manage Devices.

[https://support.switch-bot.com/hc/article_attachments/45117910036631](https://support.switch-bot.com/hc/article_attachments/45117910036631)

Control Fire TV

**On Hub 4**:From the Hub 4 home screen, tap the KATA button to open the device list, then select Fire TV. Use the knob and buttons to navigate, and the shortcut buttons for Play/Pause, Home, Back and Menu.

**By voice**: Say "Hello, KATA" or press and hold the KATA button for 1 second, then say one of the commands below. Replace <device name> with the name you gave your Fire TV in the app.

Action Voice command Mute "Mute <device name>." Unmute "Unmute <device name>." Volume up "Turn up the volume on <device name>." Volume down "Turn down the volume on <device name>." Pause "Pause on <device name>." Resume playback "Resume playback on <device name>."

### How do I unlock a lock with a voice PIN on Hub 4?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 17

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Hub 4

Add a compatible SwitchBot lock to the SwitchBot app before controlling it with Hub 4.

Step 1. Pair the lock

1. Check that the lock is in the SwitchBot app.
2. On the Hub 4 device page, tap **Manage Screen → Manage Devices → + → Import Existing Appliances**. Select the lock and tap **Pair Now**.
3. Keep your phone, lock, and Hub 4 close together until pairing is complete.

Step 2. Set a PIN

Open the lock’s **Instructions** page and tap **Unlock by Voice**. Enter the four-digit PIN twice and save it.

Step 3. Unlock by voice

Wake Hub 4 and say the lock name and PIN. For example, say “Unlock the front door. The PIN is ‹your four-digit PIN›.” Once the PIN is verified, Hub 4 says “Unlocked.”

Troubleshooting

- **Hub 4 didn’t catch the command.** Wake it again and repeat the full lock name and PIN.
- **Incorrect PIN.** Check the PIN and try again.

Before using voice unlock, make sure Hub 4 is online and connected to the lock. You can also use voice commands to lock the door or check its status.

What to do What you can say Lock “Lock the front door.” Unlock “Unlock the front door.” Check lock status “Is the front door locked?”

### How do I adjust the voice volume on Hub 4?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 17

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Hub 4

On the home screen, turn the knob clockwise to raise the voice volume or counterclockwise to lower it.

In the SwitchBot app, you can also go to **Hub 4 → Settings → Voice Settings → Voice Volume** and choose **Mute**, **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**.

Requires firmware version 13.20.12 or later.

- **When Voice Volume is set to Mute**, spoken responses, the microphones, and voice conversations are disabled, and the red microphone indicator stays lit. Switch back to Low, Medium, or High to restore voice features based on the permissions you previously granted. The red light goes out once the microphones turn on. Voice Volume does not affect alarm or doorbell volume.
- **When Do Not Disturb is on**, the screen turns off after 10 seconds of inactivity. Enabled wake-up feedback and voice conversations can still play, and alarms sound as usual. Doorbell alerts, temperature and humidity alerts, lock notifications, and button and knob sounds are muted.

### What if Hub 4 doesn’t understand me or can’t find a device?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 17

**Applies to:** SwitchBot Hub 4

Include the room, device, and action in one sentence. If Hub 4 still has trouble, check these items in order.

1. **Check the names.** Use the exact device and room names from the SwitchBot app. If multiple devices share a name, include the room, as in “living room light.”
2. **Check that Hub 4 is online.** Weather, speech recognition, and most device control require an internet connection.
3. **Check that the device is online and added to Hub 4.** Bluetooth devices, IR appliances, and locks must be paired and synced with this Hub 4.
4. **Check that the device supports the command.** Supported commands vary by model. See the voice command list in the appendix.

To see what Hub 4 recognized, open **Hub 4 → Settings → Voice Settings → Conversation History** in the app.
