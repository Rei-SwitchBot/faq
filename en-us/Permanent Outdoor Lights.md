## SwitchBot Permanent Outdoor Lights

---
### Does the 30 m SwitchBot Permanent Outdoor Lights kit work with fewer sections?

Last Updated: 2026 Nov 18

Yes. If 30 m is too long for your space, you can install just three, four, or five sections.

If you want to leave some of the connected lights off, open the device settings in the SwitchBot app and select how many lights you want to turn on.
