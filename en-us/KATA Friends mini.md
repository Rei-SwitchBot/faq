### What Is “4G Preferred”?

Last Updated: 2026 Oct 26

- **Related Products:** KATA Friends Mini

When **4G Preferred**is enabled, KATA Friends Mini will prioritize the 4G mobile network for its internet connection, even when Wi-Fi is available.

This feature is available only on the **Wi-Fi + 4G model** and requires the corresponding value-added service subscription.

### What Are Value-Added Services, and Which Plan Should I Choose?

Last Updated: 2026 Oct 15

- **Related Products:** KATA Friends Mini

1. What are value-added services?

Value-added services are optional paid subscriptions that enable AI voice conversations with KATA.

AI conversations consume KATA’s daily **Energy Points**. The amount of conversation time available each day depends on your plan. Local interactions, such as petting KATA or watching its expressions, are not limited by your subscription plan.

2. What services are available for the Wi-Fi and Wi-Fi + 4G models?

Product model Service Wi-Fi model Provides AI conversation services. A Wi-Fi connection is required. Wi-Fi + 4G model Provides AI conversation services. A subscription also enables 4G connectivity, allowing you to continue chatting where Wi-Fi is unavailable but a supported 4G network is available.

Both models offer the following plans:

- **Basic Plan:** 20 minutes of AI conversation per device per day
- **Premium Plan:** 60 minutes of AI conversation per device per day

You can select a plan for one, two, or four devices. The conversation allowance is calculated separately for each device.

3. Can subscriptions be used with both models?

No. A plan for the Wi-Fi model can only be used with Wi-Fi-only devices. A plan for the Wi-Fi + 4G model can only be used with Wi-Fi + 4G devices.

Select the correct product model when subscribing. Plans cannot be shared between the two models, even when the devices are registered to the same account.

4. How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Open the value-added service management page in the app, select the plan you want, and follow the on-screen instructions. When the change takes effect depends on your mobile platform.

### What Is Digital Life for KATA Friends Mini?

Last Updated: 2026 Oct 15

- **Related Products:** KATA Friends Mini

1. What is Digital Life?

You can think of Digital Life as KATA’s growth profile. It stores KATA’s name, personality, interaction memories, and the growth records you have created together.

2. What is Digital Life used for?

When you replace your device, you can restore the saved Digital Life to the new device and continue your companionship with KATA.

Digital Life allows KATA to remember what you call each other, your preferences, and moments from your interactions. Over time, KATA will gradually develop its own personality through these experiences.

3. How long is Digital Life retained?

When removing the device, select **Save Digital Life** to preserve its growth profile for **two years from the date the device is unlinked or removed**.

Please add a new device and restore Digital Life within this period. It cannot be restored after the retention period expires.

Each saved profile can only be restored once. If you do not select **Save Digital Life** when removing the device, the associated data will be deleted.
